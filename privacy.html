Privacy Policy – Adi T Publisher

Last updated: October 21, 2026

“Adi T Publisher” is a private publishing tool operated by Erik Talkov, who releases music as the artist Adi T. It schedules and publishes Adi T’s own music promo videos to Adi T’s own social media accounts (Pinterest, Facebook, Threads, Bluesky and X). The tool is not offered to the public, and no third party can sign up or use it.

Controller

Erik Talkov (Adi T), Germany
E-mail: prodaditbusiness@gmail.com

What data the tool processes

  • The OAuth access token and refresh token issued by each platform when the account owner (Adi T) connects their own account.
  • Basic profile information returned by the platform (account name, profile picture, account ID) to show which account is connected.
  • For Pinterest: the list of the account owner’s own boards, so a target board can be selected.
  • The videos, titles, descriptions and links created by Adi T that are published through the tool.

The tool does not collect passwords, session cookies or any data about other Pinterest users, and it does not read, analyze or store other people’s Pins, boards or profiles.

How the data is used

Tokens are used only to create Pins and posts on the account owner’s own accounts at the times the owner has scheduled. No data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising or used to train any model.

Where the data is stored

All data is stored on infrastructure controlled by the operator. Uploaded videos are kept only as long as needed for publishing.

Retention and deletion

Disconnecting an account in the tool deletes its tokens. Access can also be revoked at any time in the platform’s settings (for Pinterest: Settings → Security and logins → Apps). On request, all stored data is deleted within 30 days.

Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) you have the right to access, rectify, erase and restrict the processing of your personal data, the right to data portability and the right to object. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.
